In-app updates, straight from GitHub Releases.

* Settings → 更新 → 检查更新 looks up the latest release, shows its notes, and
  downloads the APK with a progress bar; the system installer takes over from there.
* The app only touches the network when you tap "check for updates" — reading,
  parsing and caching stay fully offline.
* After downloading, the APK's signing certificate is compared with the installed
  one. If they differ (for example when the installed build came from F-Droid),
  the app explains it instead of leaving you with the system's bare
  "app not installed" message.
* If "install unknown apps" is not granted yet, the app jumps straight to its
  permission page.
