Added text highlights, and a single place to manage all your favourites.

* Long-press a paragraph while reading to save it as a highlight
* A new Favourites screen (star icon on the shelf) collects both kinds of
  favourites in one place, split into two tabs: highlights and books
* Tap a highlight to jump back to where it came from in the book; it stores the
  whole paragraph even when the paragraph is split across two pages
* Highlights survive removing a book from the shelf, since a highlight is worth
  keeping on its own
