Shelf, sorting and reading refinements.

* Recent-book cards now carry a star: tap it to favourite or unfavourite without
  opening the book.
* Shelf order is fixed by file name again — reading a book no longer moves it to
  the top of its group, so everything stays where you expect it.
* Locking the reader no longer shows the "locked · long-press to unlock" chip
  (it covered the last line). Long-press anywhere on the page to unlock; a short
  toast confirms the lock.
* The "auto-scrolling" bar is gone too: starting auto-scroll now hides the
  toolbars automatically, and tapping the middle of the screen brings them back
  so you can stop it.
