Big-book performance and heat fixes.

* Pagination results are now cached per layout/settings, so showing or hiding
  the toolbar and re-entering a book no longer re-paginates the whole text
  (for a 11-million-character book that used to mean another minute of full load).
* Pagination pauses while the app is in the background and runs at background
  thread priority — the phone no longer stays hot after leaving the reader.
* HTML-to-text conversion was rewritten as a single pass (previously five regex
  passes over the whole book, the main source of GC pressure while parsing).
  Byte-for-byte identical output, verified against the old implementation.
* The layout step now says "正在排版…" instead of a bare spinner, so it is clear
  the app is not stuck.
