Fixed two problems in page-turning mode.

* Auto page turn could stop halfway between two pages, so the screen showed the
  bottom of one page above the top of the next. Re-pagination (which happens when
  the toolbars hide) used to cancel the turn animation mid-flight
* Hiding the toolbars could leave a large blank area at the bottom of the page
  while pagination was being recomputed. Pagination is now cancellable, so a
  superseded pass stops immediately instead of competing for CPU with the one
  that actually matters
* Horizontal rules were not counted while paginating, so pages broke slightly
  earlier than necessary
