Fixed: large MOBI books (tens of MB) appeared stuck on "loading" forever.

* The PalmDOC decompressor buffered output in a boxed Byte list — a 25 MB book
  allocated tens of millions of tiny objects and the garbage collector made the
  parse take effectively forever. It now uses a growable primitive byte buffer
  with zero per-byte allocation, and decompression finishes in seconds.
* The app now requests a larger heap, since parsing a big book keeps the source
  bytes, the decompressed text and the extracted strings in memory at once.
