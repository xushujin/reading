Fixed the toolbar not toggling, and selecting text saving the wrong passage.

* Tapping the text works again. The previous attempt made the text a text field to
  support selection, but a text field handles gestures on its own internal node, so
  the tap handler underneath never fired. Text rendering is back, and selection is
  now handled directly
* Long-press and drag to select, then tap the button that appears to save exactly
  what you highlighted. Previously the saved passage could differ from what was
  highlighted, because the selection range was being tracked by the text field
