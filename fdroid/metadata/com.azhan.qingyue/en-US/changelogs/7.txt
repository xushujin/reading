Fixed auto page turn sometimes stalling.

* Auto page turn could stop in the middle of a book while the UI still showed it
  as running; toggling it off and on was the only way to resume. The page turn
  animation is protected by a mutex and gets pre-empted whenever pagination changes,
  which raises a cancellation exception. That was being mistaken for "this coroutine
  was cancelled", so the loop driving auto page turn exited silently while the
  auto-turn flag stayed on
