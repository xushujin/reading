Fixed: page turning now works while the reading lock is on.

* In 1.3.6 the reading lock also disabled horizontal paging, so a locked page
  could not be turned at all. Paging and scrolling are now left untouched.
* The lock still ignores the system back gesture (including gesture navigation)
  and single taps that would show or hide the toolbar, and text selection stays
  disabled. Long-press the lock chip at the bottom to unlock.
