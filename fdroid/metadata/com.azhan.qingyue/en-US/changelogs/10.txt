Select the exact text you want to save, and a tidier shelf.

* Long-press a paragraph to start a selection, drag the handles to pick exactly the
  passage you want, then tap the button that appears to save it
* The shelf no longer repeats a favourites group; the star button in the top bar
  opens the favourites screen, which is the single place for both highlights and books
* Book covers without artwork now get a colour gradient derived from the title,
  instead of a grey box with a single character
