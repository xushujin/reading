The reading area now has a single fixed (full-screen) height.

* Toolbars, the lock bar and the auto-scroll bar became overlays: showing or
  hiding them no longer changes the content height, so page layout is computed
  once and stays valid — no more full re-pagination when toggling the toolbar
  (for an 11-million-character book that used to take tens of seconds per toggle).
* Scrolling mode compensates with bottom padding so the last line can scroll
  clear of the bottom bar while it is shown.
* Trade-off, chosen deliberately: while the toolbars are visible they cover the
  first and last lines of the page; hide them to see the full page.
