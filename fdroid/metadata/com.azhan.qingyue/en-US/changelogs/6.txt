Fixed auto page turn and back navigation in the reader.

* Auto page turn could stop between two pages, showing the right half of one page
  beside the left half of the next. The turn animation was being cancelled halfway:
  the effect that drives it keyed on the current page, which changes mid-animation,
  so it restarted itself and killed its own animation. It now runs as a loop that
  only restarts when auto page turn is toggled
* Pressing back (including the back gesture) while the reading settings or chapter
  panel was open used to leave the reader and return to the library. Back now closes
  the panel first
