Tapping a highlight now expands it in place instead of jumping to the book.

* Tapping a highlight shows the full text right there. A highlight is a complete
  passage on its own, so there is no need to open the book just to read it
* Long-press and drag on the text to select it, then tap the button to save exactly
  what you highlighted. This replaces the previous text-field approach, which
  swallowed taps and could save a different passage than the one highlighted
