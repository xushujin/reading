Fixed the reader toolbar not hiding, and not coming back after auto page turn stopped.

* Tapping the text no longer toggled the toolbar. Making the text selectable meant
  switching to a text field, which swallows taps even when read-only, so the tap
  handler underneath never fired. Taps are now detected ahead of it, and long presses
  are still left alone so selecting text keeps working
* Because of the same cause, the toolbar could not be brought back after auto page
  turn stopped - it hides itself automatically, and only a tap could restore it
